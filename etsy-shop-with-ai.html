The honest part

“Isn't Etsy saturated now?”

For copy-paste shops, yes. Thousands of sellers listed the same AI printables and wondered why nothing sold.

But buyers didn't leave. Etsy had 86.6 million active buyers in early 2026, and the number grew again for the first time in two years. What changed is what Etsy rewards: original products, natural titles, honest AI labels, fair shipping and listings buyers actually click.

This course teaches the 2026 version of Etsy, not the 2021 one. AI does the heavy lifting. You stay in charge of quality.
